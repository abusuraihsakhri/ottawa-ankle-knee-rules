Clinical limitations

  • For adults aged 18 or older only in this application.
  • A positive rule indicates need for radiography, not a confirmed fracture.
  • A negative rule does not exclude ligament, tendon, neurovascular or other significant injury.
  • Consider urgent assessment for deformity, open injury, neurovascular deficit or other red flags regardless of the rule.
  • Four-step criterion requires inability at both time points; limping alone does not qualify.
